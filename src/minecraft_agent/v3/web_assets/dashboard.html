Who is Kei?

Someone to talk to.
Someone who can help.

Kei is an open-source Discord chatbot and Minecraft assistant. She chats in DMs or public channels, remembers meaningful things, and helps look after servers when granted permission by the owner.

Why make this?

Because servers don't fix their own lag.
And you need someone to listen, too.

You are already on Discord. Ask Kei to check the server, look through logs or handle a task right in the conversation. Everyday chat and server work can live in the same place.
