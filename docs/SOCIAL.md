# Social, memory and privacy / Nói chuyện cho ra con người

## What happens by default

- DM, mention, reply or `kei` can address the bot. Open-source trigger names are configurable.
- Ambient participation is **off**. `/social ambient free` enables the guild; `whitelist`
  enables selected channels. Admin permission is required. `off` leaves direct calls working.
- A response can be a normal line, a short line, 👍, ❤️ or silence. An emoji is not someone
  pressing a nonexistent Continue button.
- Relationship state influences tone, not server permissions. Being her favourite menace
  does not grant shell access.

Private deployment may configure Kei-Kei, Keys, Keys Keys and Cây. Learned nicknames retain
their source and context. A nickname candidate is not proof the speaker addressed her.

## Controls

| Command | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `/persona auto/soft/spicy` | Keep the old style, stronger style, or adapt naturally |
| `/social dm on/off` | Opt in/out of unsolicited social DMs |
| `/social timezone Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` | Quiet hours use your IANA timezone |
| `/privacy strict/discreet` | Cross-context sharing preference |
| `/memory show` | Inspect selected memories privately |
| `/memory forget` | Forget an item, current context, or all personal contexts |
| `/debug off/minimal/advanced` | Existing job status controls |

Social DMs need a reason, are capped at three per day, at least four hours apart, and
stop after an unanswered initiative. Quiet hours default to 22:00–08:00. Operational
alerts are separate; a crashing server is not a social check-in.

## What lasts

Raw chat lasts 90 days by default. Selected warm episodes retain a summary and useful
original excerpts until forgotten: nickname origins, distinctive exchanges, shared moments,
reconciliation and commitments. Routine tool noise is not automatically a treasured memory.

Incremental review runs about every six hours; a daily pass at 04:00 in the configured
timezone consolidates changed conversations. Retrieval is scoped and relevance-ranked.
No external vector service is required.

Familiarity, trust, warmth, playfulness, tension and confidence are internal context, not
real feelings or a scoreboard. Repeating a message does not farm affection. Time can soften
short-term tension without deleting a friendship's history.

## Sharing

`discreet` permits a short, low-risk hint across contexts: a preference or an inside joke.
It does not quote DMs, reveal sensitive details, export restricted-channel history or tell
someone else's private story. `strict` keeps memories in their source audience.

Forgetting invalidates derived memories, episodes, aliases and relevant summaries, including
retained excerpts. Later consolidation cannot rebuild them from tombstoned sources.
Minimal task/action audit remains separate from conversational memory.
